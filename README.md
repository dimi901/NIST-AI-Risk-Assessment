## Objective

I completed an AI risk assessment, where I developed a hypothetical AI-powered healthcare diagnostics system for the fictional company HealthAI Diagnostics. I applied the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) to identify, assess, and mitigate risks such as bias, security, privacy, and operational challenges. This project enhanced my ability to design ethical AI systems, manage risks, and communicate effectively with stakeholders, while ensuring transparency and trust in AI technologies.

### Skills Learned

- **AI Risk Management Framework Application:** Gained hands-on experience applying the NIST AI RMF to identify, assess, and mitigate risks in an AI system.
- **Bias Detection and Mitigation:** Learned to identify potential biases in AI systems and implement strategies to ensure fairness and inclusivity.
- **Cybersecurity and Privacy Protections:** Developed skills in securing AI systems through encryption, access controls, and compliance with privacy regulations like HIPAA.
- **Stakeholder Communication and Transparency:** Enhanced ability to communicate AI system functionality and risks to stakeholders, ensuring trust and accountability.
- **Continuous Monitoring and Improvement:** Acquired knowledge of strategies for ongoing system evaluation, updates, and adaptation to emerging risks and advancements.

## About HealthAI Diagnostics

HealthAI Diagnostics is a healthcare technology company that develops an AI-powered diagnostic system to assist medical professionals in identifying diseases and recommending treatment plans. The system, called DiagnoAI, uses machine learning algorithms to analyze patient data, including medical history, lab results, imaging scans, and genetic information, to provide accurate and timely diagnoses.
